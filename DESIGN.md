## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a forward-leaning cultural salon: intellectually sharp, youthful, and uncompromisingly premium. It speaks to high-achieving creatives, cultural operators, and modern leaders who reject sterile corporate networking in favor of authentic intellectual community. 

The aesthetic sits at the intersection of avant-garde print journalism and high-end digital publishing. It embraces generous spatial intervals, assertive typographic hierarchy, asymmetric layouts, hairline structural boundaries, and human-centric documentary photography. Surfaces remain light, clean, and disciplined, allowing electric purple accents and deep typography to hold immediate focal power without resorting to synthetic SaaS gradients or bubbly, juvenile abstractions.

## Colors

The palette balances sharp contrast with architectural calm:

- **Canvas & Layering**: The primary foundation is pure crisp White (`#FFFFFF`), paired with a gallery-grade off-white (`#FAFAFA`) to establish rhythmic sectional pacing and soft framing without creating muddiness.
- **Accents**: 
  - **Royal Electric Purple (`#6C3BFF`)**: Serves as the primary operational hue, reserved strictly for primary interactive anchors, focused states, and key editorial emphasis.
  - **Deep Purple (`#4520A8`)**: Acts as the grounding chromatic tone for elevated interactions, active states, and high-impact typographic counterpoints.
  - **Soft Purple Tint (`#F0EBFF`)**: Provides a delicate, warm-violet field for metadata badges, pill tags, category highlights, and active container fills.
- **Text & Structure**: 
  - Primary text relies on a rich, warm near-black (`#17131F`) to maintain deep contrast without the clinical harshness of `#000000`.
  - Secondary metadata, captions, and secondary actions utilize an elegant muted slate (`#6F6878`).
  - Structural separation is governed by subtle, architectural borders (`#EAE6F2`), delivering definition without visual noise.

## Typography

The type system stages a dialogue between technical discipline and contemporary warmth:

- **Display & Editorial Headlines (`Space Grotesk`)**: Provides an intentional, structured posture reminiscent of contemporary high-fashion and cultural art direction. The negative tracking tightens with scale to create punchy, poster-like authority.
- **Body & Operational Interface (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers geometric clarity and warm, open apertures that guarantee comfortable long-form reading across mobile and desktop.
- **Editorial Micro-Tags (`Space Grotesk`)**: Rendered in uppercase with generous letter-spacing (`0.08em`) to demarcate dates, edition numbers, index coordinates, and cultural tags cleanly without overwhelming neighboring body copy.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an asymmetric 12-column editorial grid engineered to break the monotony of conventional 3-card marketing sites:

- **Grid Architecture**: 
  - **Desktop (≥1200px)**: 12 columns with 40px (`2.5rem`) gutters and 64px (`4rem`) outer margins. Content utilizes asymmetric balance—e.g., 5-column editorial text paired against 7-column multi-aspect image clusters.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8 columns with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and 40px (`2.5rem`) margins.
  - **Mobile (<768px)**: 4 columns with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 20px (`1.25rem`) outer margins.
- **Vertical Intervals**: Large vertical padding (`space-xl` scaled to 6rem–9rem on desktop) creates decisive breathing room between narrative chapters, accentuating editorial focus and eliminating visual claustrophobia.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids generic, blurred floating drop-shadows. Depth is derived from tactile layering, sharp linear division, and translucent atmospheric shifts:

- **Hairline Delimitation**: Hierarchy is delineated primarily through precise 1px borders in `#EAE6F2`.
- **Architectural Grounding**: Cards and interactive elements sit flat on `#FFFFFF` or `#FAFAFA` surfaces. Upon hover or active focus, cards transition smoothly without upward physical pop; instead, borders shift from `#EAE6F2` to `#6C3BFF`, paired with an ultra-subtle, un-blurred directional offset (e.g., `box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px 0px #F0EBFF`).
- **Frosted Overlays**: Sticky navigation bars and floating context menus use a crisp frosted treatment (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` backdrop with `blur(12px)`) combined with an explicit bottom boundary in `#EAE6F2`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a soft, architectural shape language:

- **Base Curvature (`0.25rem` / 4px)**: Applied to foundational UI elements like input controls, editorial index cards, and framed photography containers to preserve structured, print-journalism sharpness.
- **Pill Attributes (Exceptions)**: Badges, cultural categories, and status chips are permitted full pill curvature (`9999px`) to create an intentional graphic contrast against the angular structure of typography and media framing.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button**: Solid Royal Electric Purple (`#6C3BFF`) fill with pure White (`#FFFFFF`) text in `label-lg`. Padding: `12px 24px`. Border-radius: `4px`. Hover state triggers an immediate shift to Deep Purple (`#4520A8`) with zero vertical translation.
- **Secondary / Outlined Button**: Transparent fill with a 1px border in `#17131F` and near-black text. Hover transforms background to `#17131F` and text to `#FFFFFF`.
- **Tertiary / Subtle Action**: Background in `#F0EBFF` with `#6C3BFF` text. On hover, background shifts to a 15% opacity tint of primary purple.

### Badges & Chips
- Scaled using `label-mono-tag`. Background: `#F0EBFF`, text: `#4520A8`. Fully rounded (`9999px`) with horizontal padding of `10px` and vertical padding of `4px`. Borderless by default, maintaining high legibility and refined scale.

### Cards & Editorial Modules
- Built on `#FFFFFF` or `#FAFAFA` backgrounds with a continuous 1px `#EAE6F2` border. No rounded card bubbles: corners remain disciplined at `4px`. Imagery within cards occupies edge-to-edge slots separated by a 1px border divider.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- Minimalist architectural styling. Crisp 1px `#EAE6F2` border on `#FFFFFF` background. Padding: `14px 16px`. Active focus transitions the border to `#6C3BFF` with an immediate, razor-thin `0 0 0 1px #6C3BFF` ring—no diffused, cloudy glow. Placeholder text in `#6F6878`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square 18px boxes with 2px corner radius. Inactive: `#FFFFFF` fill with 1px `#EAE6F2` border. Checked: `#6C3BFF` fill containing a sharp white checkmark glyph.

### Editorial Photography Containers
- Imagery is treated with humanistic documentary grading. Frames employ an aspect-ratio-locked box with an optional inset hairline border (`inset 0 0 0 1px rgba(23, 19, 31, 0.08)`), pairing high visual craft with intentional architectural restraint.
